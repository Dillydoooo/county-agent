2. Southern Oregon Sanitation Franchise Sale 
Chair Smith opened discussion regarding the proposed sale of Southern Oregon Sanitation (SOS) to 
Waste Connections. Smith stated for the record that the Board of Commissioners had no prior 
knowledge of the proposed transaction before the June 8, 2026 meeting and likewise had no knowledge 
of the transaction at the time recent rate adjustments associated with the Kirby Transfer Station were 
considered.  
Trent Carpenter, representing Southern Oregon Sanitation, informed the Board that the Carpenter 
family had decided to sell the company after approximately eighty years of operation. Carpenter 
explained that the decision was based on a variety of family and business considerations and that 
discussions with Waste Connections began after the family determined it was time to transition 
ownership. Carpenter reported that Waste Connections had agreed to retain local management and staff 
and that customers should experience no interruption in service. Carpenter stated that collection 
schedules, customer service operations, company branding, and franchise obligations would remain 
unchanged following the anticipated August 1, 2026 closing date.  
Brian White, Division Vice President for Waste Connections in Oregon, introduced himself and 
provided an overview of Waste Connections' operations throughout Oregon. White explained that the 
company intends to maintain the local operating structure currently utilized by Southern Oregon 
Sanitation and emphasized that customer service functions and day-to-day operational decisions would 
continue to be handled locally. White stated that the acquisition would not alter the existing franchise 
agreements or rate-setting processes and that any future rate requests would continue to be reviewed 
through the same regulatory and public processes currently in place.  
Commissioners discussed the potential impact of the transaction on ratepayers, franchise 
administration, and solid waste operations within Josephine County. Carpenter explained that franchise 
agreements governing solid waste collection services establish allowable profit ranges and require 
annual financial reporting and review. Carpenter and White indicated that any future rate adjustments 
would remain subject to existing franchise requirements, review by the Southern Oregon Sanitation 
Agency, and approval by the appropriate governing bodies. Both representatives stated that the 
transaction itself would not result in automatic rate increases and that all existing contractual 
obligations would remain in effect following the sale.  
Discussion also occurred regarding potential future revisions to local franchise agreements. Carpenter 
noted that local governments have expressed interest in updating and standardizing portions of the 
franchise agreements to remove outdated references, improve consistency among jurisdictions, and 
provide additional clarification regarding franchise enforcement and exemptions. Commissioners 
discussed concerns related to illegal dumping, cleanup efforts within rural areas of the County, and

opportunities to improve coordination between solid waste providers and local governments. Carpenter 
acknowledged those concerns and indicated that additional discussions regarding franchise 
modernization and enforcement could occur in the future.  
Commissioners further discussed ownership of the Dry Creek Landfill by Waste Connections and 
asked whether the acquisition could affect disposal costs or franchise rates. White explained that 
Southern Oregon Sanitation would continue operating under existing disposal agreements and that rates 
charged under those agreements would remain subject to contractual provisions and regulatory 
oversight. Carpenter noted that Southern Oregon Sanitation currently operates under a long-term 
disposal agreement and that landfill pricing remains subject to review by the governing entities 
responsible for oversight.  
The Board discussed the due diligence process associated with the transfer of franchise interests and 
reviewed provisions contained within the franchise agreements addressing financial capability, 
operational qualifications, and transfer requirements. White described Waste Connections' operations 
throughout Oregon and offered to provide references from other jurisdictions served by the company. 
Commissioners expressed appreciation for the information provided and indicated they would continue 
reviewing the transaction as it proceeds through the formal approval process.  
Carpenter stated that the transaction would involve the transfer of ownership of Southern Oregon 
Sanitation and related assets while maintaining existing operations, employees, and franchise 
obligations. White reiterated Waste Connections' commitment to maintaining local operations and 
community involvement following completion of the transaction. Commissioners thanked Southern 
Oregon Sanitation and Waste Connections representatives for attending the meeting and providing 
information regarding the proposed sale.
