1) Open Meeting – Gary Richardson, Budget Committee Chair 
Commissioner Richardson opened the meeting at 9:01 a.m.  
2) Declaration of Potential Conflicts of Interest 
Before departmental presentations began, Chair Richardson discussed current County staffing levels and 
reviewed information comparing active County staffing levels to proposed budgeted full-time equivalent 
positions (FTEs). Richardson explained that County staffing levels during recent months had ranged between 
approximately 392 and 400 filled positions, while the proposed Fiscal Year 2026–2027 budget reflected 
approximately 425 budgeted FTE positions. Richardson stated that the Board had previously requested that 
departments prepare budget presentations based primarily on currently filled positions rather than 
maintaining vacant positions within proposed departmental budgets unless specifically justified through 
operational requests. 
 
Richardson distributed comparative staffing information to Budget Committee members and stated that 
several departments included budgeted positions exceeding currently filled staffing levels. Richardson 
explained that the information was intended to provide additional context for departmental budget 
discussions and future staffing considerations during the budget process. 
3) Department Presentations: 
a. Facilities - Ryan Johnson  
Ryan Johnson, Facilities Director, presented the proposed Facilities Department budget and provided an 
overview of departmental operations and responsibilities. The department is responsible for maintenance and 
repair of County buildings and properties, communication and security systems, custodial support, and 
capital improvement projects. Discussions occurred regarding staffing levels, facility maintenance needs, 
reserve funding, long-term infrastructure planning, and future capital improvement priorities. 
 
b. Transit - Scott Chancey-Exhibit 1 PowerPoint Presentation  
Scott Chancey presented the proposed Transit Department budget and provided an overview of departmental 
operations, ridership trends, grant funding, and transportation services provided throughout Josephine 
County. Exhibit 1 – Transit PowerPoint Presentation.  Discussions occurred regarding federal and state 
transportation funding, vehicle replacement planning, operational costs, and future sustainability 
considerations for transit services within Josephine County 
 
c. Recreation – Tamra Martin-Exhibit 2 PowerPoint Presentation  
Tamra Martin presented the proposed Recreation Department budget and reviewed operations associated 
with the Fairgrounds and Recreation Department facilities. Martin discussed Fairgrounds maintenance 
projects, event coordination, operational funding, and long-term facility planning considerations. Exhibit 2 – 
Recreation PowerPoint Presentation. Discussion occurred regarding Fairgrounds capital projects, event 
revenue, deferred maintenance, and preservation of Fairgrounds facilities and infrastructure 
 
d. Airports – Michael Crissafulli  
Michael Crissafulli presented the proposed Airports Department budget and discussed airport operations, 
lease revenue, infrastructure maintenance, and future airport development considerations. Discussion 
included airport maintenance responsibilities, lease management, operational funding, and ongoing planning 
associated with airport facilities and operations. Budget Committee members discussed airport operational 
structure, infrastructure planning, and future budget considerations associated with airport operations and 
maintenance responsibilities 
 
4) Budget Officer answers questions/provides requested information 
Arthur O’Hare responded to Budget Committee questions and provided additional clarification regarding 
budget structure, departmental funding, staffing calculations, and reserve considerations discussed during 
departmental presentations. 
5) Public Comment (limit of three minutes each) 
No public testimony was provided. 
6) Confirm next scheduled meeting.  
May 27, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. 
The meeting adjourned at 12:31 p.m.

Department of Recreation
Prepared by: Tamra Martin
PURPOSE:
Fairgrounds: to provide the citizens of Josephine County a high functioning multi-use facility year-round filled 
with opportunities for education, agriculture, social engagement, entertainment and economic growth while 
being a resource for emergency response support and enhancing tourism, all the while honoring our County’s 
traditions.
Parks: provide safe, accessible and well-maintained public lands that support recreation, economic 
development through tourism, environmental stewardship and quality of life for our community.
Granite Hill Cemetery: to honor and preserve the cemetery’s rich historic legacy while managing it’s 40 acres. 
Ensuring accurate records, public access, safety, maintenance and community service for future generations.
