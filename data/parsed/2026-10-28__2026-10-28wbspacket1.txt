Josephine County Fair Board Meeting  
September 15, 2026 
Josephine County Fairgrounds Floral Bldg 
1451 Fairgrounds Rd., Grants Pass, Or. 97527 
Present: Board Chair: Dean Saxon, Vice Chair: Karl Haeckler, Kim Gasperson, Jen McGowan 
Trent Carpenter, Arthur O’Hare and Tamra Martin Fairgrounds Manager  
These are meeting minutes only.  Only text enclosed in quotation marks (“) reports speaker’s 
exact words.  
1. Call to order 
Pursuant to notice through the media and in conformance with the Public Meeting Law, 
Dean Saxon called the meeting to order at 6:00 pm.  
2.  Agenda 
Karl Haeckler made a motion to approve the Agenda for September 15, 2026, and Kim 
Gasperson 2nd the motion.  The motion passed unanimously.  
3. Minutes 
Karl Haeckler made a motion to approve the minutes from August 18, 2026, and Jen 
McGowan 2nd the motion.  The motion passed unanimously. 
4. General Public Input: Guest in attendance: Frank Boothby and Lonnie & Debbie 
Johnson: Online: Anne Byers, Deb Berg, Kaci Williams, Marcia Pratt, Libby Watts 
a. Lonnie Johnson: Spoke on behalf of Kiwana’s PNW to share his 
appreciation for the Fair Board and the partnership for Fair parking. 
b. Kaci Williams: Wants clarity for livestock rules around shows 
c. Deb Berg: was contacted after the auction from Montgomery Meats by 
error. 
5. Reports 
a. 4H/OSU – Frank Boothby discussed their move to the RCC campus for their 
office space and hiring 2 new staff members.  Also brought up birthdates for 
livestock shows and wants clear expectations prior to Fair. 
b. Auction Committee -Jen McGowan- discussed the updates to the manual for 
next year’s Fair and is working with processors to schedule 2027. 
c. Facilities Committee- Dean Saxon – no meeting, nothing to report 
d. Friends of the Fairgrounds Report- Dean Saxon- nothing new to report 
e. Director’s Report 
1. Grounds Report- Tamra reported that the staff has shifted gears 
from Fair clean up to upcoming Fall and Winter reservations.  
Focused on cleaning the buildings for a busy event season.  Horse 
racing is back on the grounds for their Fall Racing season.  She 
encouraged the Board to go visit a race day to see the 
improvements that have been made and the displays of the 
proposed improvements. 
Item 7b2
2. Fair 2027: 
1. Tamra would like to rearrange how the Fair is laid out to 
freshen up the look of the Fair.  She also asked the Board to 
start considering the theme for next year’s Fair as 
mentioned that it’s the 100th year of the County owning the 
Fairgrounds. 
3. Upcoming Events:  
1. Trunk or Treat: October 31st Halloween does fall on a 
Saturday this year and is expecting a large crowd.  Lonnie 
confirmed that Key Club will be volunteering to help hand 
out candy and Tamra asked the Board to show up Saturday 
morning to help park cars and participate in the event. 
2. Last Minute Gift Sale: December 19th is the sale date.  
Tamra is requesting help from the Board to help set up in 
the days leading up, sale day for visitor interaction and tear 
down at the end of the event. 
6. Business 
a. Oregon Fairs Association: Tamra spoke about the Oregon Fairs 
Association and the purpose and impact that they have on all County Fairs.  
She invited all the Fair Board to attend the upcoming convention where 
there will be legislative updates and education opportunities. 
b. Fee Review & Discussion: The County is getting ready to review the fees 
in their annual process.  In addition to the previous fees that the Board has 
approved, Tamra proposed: 
• Monthly arena rental fee from $30 to $35 for the month.  
• Fair Office Rental Fee: $100 
• Vendor Penalty Fee- Board discussed this proposed fee.  Discussed 
calling it Contract Non-Compliance Fee with a range of $50-$250. 
• 
Open Class Livestock Fee- $10 This will not pertain to Youth that have 
already paid a 10$ Prefair fee for this animal if registered as a backup.  
Trent Carpenter made a motion to approve the summary of fee changes as presented with the 
exception of changing the Vendor penalty fee to Contract Non-Compliance Fee and Open Class 
Fee is changed to the Open Class Livestock Fee, and Jen McGowan 2nd the motion.  The motion 
passed unanimously. 
7. Other Business 
8. Next Fair Board Meeting: October 20, 2026 @ 6:00 p.m. 
Motion was made to adjourn at 6:58 pm by Jen McGowan and 2nd by Karl Haeckler.  Motion passed 
unanimously.
