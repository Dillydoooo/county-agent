PUBLIC HEARING:
a. Second Public Hearing -2024 Community Development Block Grant (CDBG) Program IJ24007 -
Close-out of the Josephine County Home Repair & Rehabilitation Program
Chair Smith opened the public hearing at9:06 a.m. Hel6ne Lulich, Real Property Manager, Office of Legal Counsel,
presented the required second public hearing for close-out of the 2024 Community Development Block Grant
(CDBG) Home Repair & Rehabilitation Program,H24007 . Lulich explained that the grant was complete and the
hearing was required before the County could close the project and proceed with its next grant award. She reported
that the program began assisting residents in February 2025 and read a letter from a Cave Junction-area homeowner
expressing appreciation for the County and Home Bridging GP after the program replaced a deteriorated roof and
improved the home.
Austin Hansen, Home Bridging GP, presented program results (Exhibit 1). He reported that2l homes occupied by 29
residents were assisted, all qualifring as low- to moderate-income households, with more than $420,000 invested in
home repairs. Nine of the homes were manufactured homes located in mobile home parks. Work completed included
windows and doors, wells, accessibility ramps, structural/seismic work, gutters, septic repairs and tank replacements,
and l3 roof replacements. Hansen stated that local contractors performed the work, federal program requirements
were met, and a substantial waiting list already exists for the next grant cycle. Public testimony was then received.
Deb Berg, Josephine County, identified additional manufactured-home parks in the County and expressed
appreciation for the repair work, encouraging continued outreach to eligible residents in those communities.
Mark Jones, Josephine County, stated that several manufactured-home parks in the County contain homes in need of
repair. He encouraged broader promotion of the program and consideration of additional qualified organizations that
could become subgrantees and perform rehabilitation work in future grant cycles.
Debbie Richmond, Josephine County, praised the program and suggested that public property at the Fairgrounds
could also be considered in broader discussions regarding subsidized or low-income housing opportunities.
Eva Avery, Grants Pass, stated that she had not previously known about the program and described the needs of
residents in her 5S-and-older manufactured-home community. She encouraged additional outreach to manufactured-
home residents and raised concerns about increasing space rents. Lulich responded that the CDBG repair program
does not regulate space rents; organizations interested in participating may seek qualification through Business
Oregon as subgrantees; and County grant funds may serve eligible properties outside the City of Grants Pass because
the City receives its own HUD funding directly.
Commissioner Richardson moved to approve the close-out of the 2024 Community Development Block Grant (CDBG)
Program H24007, Josephine County Home Repair & Rehabilitation Program. Commissioner Martin seconded the motion.
Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-
0.
Chair Smith closed the public hearing at9:24 a.m.

County airports (Exhibit 2). He discussed Airport Advisory Board participation, access to airport management,
unanswered questions from airport users, and the Grants Pass Airport weather-system outage. He stated that the
outage had delayed reactivation of instrument approach and departure procedures and emphasized the potential effect

described Brett Hopper's aviation experience and involvement with the airport community.
Mark Jones, Josephine County, expressed concern about the reported wildlife hazard at the airport based on his prior
aircraft-rescue firefighting experience. He also noted that ODF had declared fire season over and thanked local fire
districts, ODF, federal agencies, and mutual-aid partners for their work during the2026 fire season.
