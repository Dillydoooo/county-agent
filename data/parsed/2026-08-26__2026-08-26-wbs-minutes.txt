3. ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION(S) IN CONSIDERATION OF: 
a. Resolution No. 2026-032 - Water/Sewer Infrastructure Extension Funds 
Wally Hicks, Legal Counsel, presented Resolution No. 2026-032 supporting Josephine County and the City of Cave 
Junction in pursuing state funding for planning and engineering related to extending water and sewer infrastructure 
toward Kirby and the lllinois Valley Airport. He explained that the County and City intend to apply to separate 
Business Oregon programs and that the resolution documents that, if both applications are successful, the 
jurisdictions will avoid assigning the same costs to both awards. The Board clarified that the immediate project is 
planning and engineering only, not construction of infrastructure. 
A motion was made and seconded to approve Resolution No. 2026-032. Upon roll call: Commissioner 
Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0. 
b. Letter of Support - City of Cave Junction Water/Sewer Infrastructure Extension Funds 
Legal Counsel presented the companion letter supporting the City of Cave Junction application. The letter is part of 
the City application package for the state infrastructure-planning program. 
Commissioner Richardson made a motion to approve the letter of 
support for the City of 
Cave Junction application. 
Seconded by Commissioner Martin. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; 
Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0. 
c. Grants Pass Airport - Temporary Runway Closure Lighting and Visual Aid 
Airport Manager Michael Crissafulli presented a recommendation to award the purchase of portable runway-closure 
markers for the Grants Pass Airport Taxiway Extension Project. Three proposals were solicited and two bids were 
received; the manufacturer, Halibrite, submitted the lowest bid at $56,691. The purchase had already been included 
in the previously approved grant agreement. The portable units unfold into large illuminated X markers placed at 
runway ends and will remain County emergency equipment after construction is completed. 
The Board discussed the planned 21 nighttime runway closures, the anticipated construction schedule, FAA 
NOT AM notification requirements, and the value of having the equipment available for unplanned closures. The 
Airport Manager stated that daytime operations will remain open during construction except as otherwise required 
and that the markers can also be transported to the lllinois Valley Airport if needed. 
Commissioner Richardson made a motion to approve the recommendation of 
award and associated contract for the 
temporary runway-closure lighting and visual-aid equipment. Seconded by Commissioner Martin. Upon roll call: 
Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0. 
d. Board Order No. 2026-046 - Designation of Josephine County Budget Officer 
Legal Counsel explained that Oregon budget law requires the County to formally designate a budget officer. 
Because the prior temporary Finance Director had left County employment and Matthew Eide is now serving as 
Finance Director, the Board was asked to designate Mr. Eide as Budget Officer in advance of the supplemental- 
budget process. 
Commissioner Martin made a motion to approve Board Order No. 2026-046 designating Matthew Eide as 
Josephine County Budget Officer. Seconded by Commissioner Richardson. Upon roll call: Commissioner 
Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0.

4. REQUESTS/COMMENTS FROM CITIZENS: 
(Each person was given three (3) minutes to speak.) 
Judy Ahrens, Josephine County, expressed concerns regarding public education, student achievement, civic 
knowledge, and the direction of public policy. She encouraged residents to attend local school board meetings and 
participate in education governance. 
Mike Pelfrey, Josephine County, asked the Board to consider a resolution or policy statement similar to one recently 
adopted by Douglas County concerning Oregon sanctuary laws. He also expressed concerns regarding crime and 
requested stronger local law-enforcement responses. 
BOARD MAY REVIEW PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS: 
Commissioner Richardson thanked Ms. Ahrens for her interest in education and discussed current Grants Pass 
School District 7 policy work and educational initiatives. Regarding the sanctuary-law request, he stated that the 
issue involves overlapping federal and state authority and that any County resolution would largely be a policy 
statement rather than a change in applicable law. He emphasized the need to avoid actions that could expose the 
County to adverse legal consequences. 
Commissioner Martin agreed that the Douglas County action was more properly viewed as a policy statement and 
noted that legal and governmental responses to that action were still developing. 
Chair Smith stated that he had obtained and reviewed the Douglas County resolution and discussed it with the 
Sheriff. He emphasized that a County statement would not change Oregon law or the legal duties of the Sheriff and 
Board, and said he did not favor placing Josephine County in legal jeopardy for an action that would not change the 
governing law.
