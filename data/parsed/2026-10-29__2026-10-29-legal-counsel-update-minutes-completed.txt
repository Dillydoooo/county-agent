2. Order No. 2026-xxx; In the Matter of Approving the Josephine County Rural Fire Protection District 
Boundary in Final Approved Form for Submission to the Department of Revenue and the County 
Assessor Pursuant to ORS 308.225 
Wally Hicks, Legal Counsel, provided an update regarding the proposed final boundary for the Josephine 
County Rural Fire Protection District. He reviewed the formation of the district by the Board of County 
Commissioners, the transfer of approximately $1.5 million in startup funding to the district, and the 
subsequent election of the district's governing board. He also reviewed the prior boundary dispute with the 
Applegate Rural Fire Protection District, the court judgment requiring the disputed Applegate coverage-
area properties to be excluded, and the amended boundary previously accepted by the Oregon Department 
of Revenue. 
Legal Counsel advised that a later review identified additional property-boundary discrepancies that now 
require correction. Because the existing boundary was established by a prior Board Order and subsequently 
incorporated into a court judgment, the County must return through that process to amend the boundary. 
The Department of Revenue has advised that a property spreadsheet alone is insufficient and that a 
professionally surveyed boundary description is required. Legal Counsel is coordinating with the district 
regarding the survey cost and supporting documentation and anticipates returning to the Board in 
approximately one month with the completed map and related letters. No formal action was taken.
