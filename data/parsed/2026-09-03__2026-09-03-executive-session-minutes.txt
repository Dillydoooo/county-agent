Recorder 
Chair Ron A. Smith called the meeting to order at 2:00 p.m. 
1. OPEN SESSION 
a. Consideration and decision regarding the dismissal or discipline of, or to hear

a. Consideration and decision regarding the dismissal or discipline of, or to hear

individual agent.
