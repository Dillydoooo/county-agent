2. Department Business 
a. Public Works 
1. Amendment for IGA with ODOT for Althouse Creek Bridge Replacement 
Rob Brandes, Public Works Director, presented the amendment to the intergovernmental agreement with 
ODOT for the Althouse Creek Bridge replacement. The project is a full bridge reconstruction funded 
primarily through the state bridge program, with approximately $2.7 million awarded and a County match 
of 10.27%, or approximately $313,000. The amendment allows ODOT to perform the right-of-way and land 
acquisition work. The Board discussed potential easements, federal funding restrictions if property 
associated with marijuana production must be acquired, anticipated project cost increases, detour needs, and 
a projected four- to six-month closure during construction. Public Works anticipates bidding in early 2027 
for construction in summer 2027. The Board also requested that Public Works continue bringing significant 
change orders back for discussion. 
Commissioner Martin moved to approve the Amendment for the IGA with ODOT for the Althouse Creek Bridge 
Replacement. Commissioner Richardson seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; 
Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0.

c. Transit 
1. ODOT Agreement 35810-1 
Scott Chancey, Transit Manager, presented ODOT Agreement 35810-1. The amendment adds an additional 
quarter of funding to bridge ODOT’s transition from a July-June fiscal year to the federal October-
September fiscal year. The funds support rural public transportation and may be used for eligible expenses 
such as preventive fleet maintenance. The local match is provided through state transit funds; no County 
General Fund dollars are required.

2. Spare Software Renewal Agreement 
Scott Chancey, Transit Manager, presented the Spare software renewal agreement (Form SL-7542). Spare is 
used for demand-response dispatching and client scheduling for elderly and disabled riders, including ADA-
related service tracking. The annual cost is approximately $50,400 and is paid with federal funds, with the 
local match provided through state transit funds. Mr. Chancey stated the software continues to meet the 
department’s needs and requested authorization to sign the renewal. 
Commissioner Martin moved to approve the Spare software renewal agreement and authorize Scott Chancey to 
sign. Commissioner Richardson seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; 
Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0.

a. Resolution 2026-035 Josephine County Economic Development Grant Advisory 
Committee - Appointments 
Staff reviewed the selection process for the five proposed members of the Josephine County Economic 
Development Grant Advisory Committee. Candidates were interviewed individually, and selections were

b. Communications Update 
Commissioner Martin provided an update on the regional emergency communications project. A recent 
meeting with Emergency Management, City of Grants Pass and fire service representatives addressed a 
broader communications system overhaul. The group is evaluating Public Safety Towers Company (PSTC), 
an independent tower developer, as one possible avenue for adding infrastructure. A coverage analysis 
identified a potential need for three additional towers beyond the County’s existing four. The Board 
discussed ownership and lease protections, locating towers to serve County public-safety needs, project 
costs, and available grant opportunities, including a Homeland Security grant and larger funding 
opportunities anticipated in 2027. No formal action was taken. 
No formal action was taken. 
c. Designate a Project Coordinator for Emergency Communication 
The Board discussed designating a single point of contact to keep the long-term regional communications 
project moving forward and coordinate the work of Emergency Management, Facilities, technical staff and 
grant resources. Nate Sanders was identified as having relevant prior experience and as a suitable 
coordinator, while technical staff would continue to be directly involved. The Board emphasized that the 
project should be developed for the benefit of Josephine County as a whole and not for a single department. 
Commissioner Richardson moved to task Nate Sanders as Project Coordinator for the Josephine Regional 
Interoperational System. Commissioner Martin seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson 
- Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0.

4. OTHER 
Commissioner Richardson provided an update on the Agricultural Education Grants. Legal Counsel and 
Finance are working on the framework, which is expected to be patterned generally after the economic 
development grant process but without an advisory committee. Applications would come directly to the 
Board. The available funding pool is $30,000 through the 4-H Service District and may support agricultural 
education opportunities such as FFA, 4-H, youth agriculture or other qualifying programs. The goal is to 
have the process ready in time for awards later this year. 
Commissioner Richardson also discussed a proposed Department Report process. Departments would 
receive a standardized packet addressing department activities, key performance indicators, goals and 
obstacles, and would submit the completed report to the Board at least one week before appearing at an 
Administrative Workshop. The Board discussed having each department report approximately every six 
months, with about 10-15 minutes for discussion and questions. The draft template will be shared with 
department heads at an upcoming leadership meeting for feedback before the schedule is finalized.
