APPROVED ON OCTOBER 7, 2026 
BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
AT THE WEEKLY BUSINESS SESSION 
Open Session: October 1, 2026 
1:30 p.m. – Board of Commissioners Conference Room 
Attending: Commissioners Ron A. Smith; Colene M. Martin; Gary L. Richardson; and Nikole 
Guzman, Recorder 
Chair Ron A. Smith called the meeting to order at 1:30 p.m. 
1. OPEN SESSION
a. To consider the employment of a public officer, employee, staff member or
individual agent.
Chair Smith announced the matter before the Board and stated that the Board would convene in Executive 
Session pursuant to ORS 192.660(2)(a) to consider the employment of a public officer, employee, staff 
member or individual agent. 
2. EXECUTIVE SESSION
a. Executive Session To consider the employment of a public officer, employee,
staff member or individual agent - ORS 192.660(2)(a).
Chair Ron Smith convened the Executive Session at 1:31 p.m. 
Additional Attendees: Natalie Eckhardt-Doupe, Interim Human Resources Director; Chrissy Ewald, 
Daily Courier 
Executive Session concluded at 2:10 p.m. 
3. OPEN SESSION CON’D
a. To consider the employment of a public officer, employee, staff member or
individual agent.
The Board reconvened in Open Session at 2:10 p.m. Chair Smith stated that no decision had been made in 
Executive Session and asked each Commissioner to identify Candidate A or Candidate B. Commissioner 
Richardson selected Candidate B; Commissioner Martin selected Candidate A; and Commissioner Smith 
selected Candidate A. Candidate A received the majority selection, 2-1. The Board directed Human 
Resources to continue with the hiring process. 
The meeting adjourned at 2:11 p.m.
