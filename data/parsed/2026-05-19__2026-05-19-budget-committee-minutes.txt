1) Open Meeting – Gary Richardson, Budget Committee Chair 
Commissioner Martin opened the meeting at 9:00 a.m. The budget committee reviewed the agenda and 
determined that there are no potential conflicts of interest on today’s agenda.  
2) Budget Officer presents Budget Message and Proposed Budget for FY 26-27 
Arthur O’Hare, Budget Officer, presented the Fiscal Year 2026–2027 Proposed Budget and delivered the 
annual Budget Message required under Oregon Budget Law. O’Hare thanked the citizen Budget Committee 
members for their participation and emphasized the importance of public representation within the County 
budget process. O’Hare explained that the Board of County Commissioners establishes budget goals and 
directives while the Budget Officer prepares the proposed budget under the Board’s direction. He further 
explained that the Budget Committee is responsible for reviewing the proposed budget, receiving public 
testimony, and making recommendations prior to formal budget adoption. 
 
O’Hare advised that the proposed Fiscal Year 2026–2027 budget complied with Oregon Budget Law 
requirements and that all County funds remained balanced. Discussion occurred regarding budget resources, 
expenditures, contingencies, beginning fund balances, ending fund balances, and reserve requirements 
established by the Board of Commissioners during prior budget workshops. O’Hare explained that the 
County maintains contingency and reserve requirements to address emergency expenditures, maintain 
adequate cash flow prior to receipt of property tax revenues, and stabilize future law enforcement funding 
needs. 
 
O’Hare reviewed overall budget figures and explained that the total proposed budget for Fiscal Year 2026–
2027 was approximately $188.9 million, representing an approximate three percent increase over the prior 
fiscal year adopted budget. He stated that operating expenditures for payroll, supplies, services, and capital 
had decreased overall by approximately $2.4 million, while County staffing levels reflected a net reduction 
of approximately 20.79 full-time equivalent positions compared to the prior fiscal year adopted budget. 
 
Discussion occurred regarding the structure of the General Fund and the County’s reliance on restricted and 
unrestricted revenues. O’Hare explained that approximately eighty-seven percent of County revenues are 
restricted funding sources and unavailable for discretionary County operations, while approximately thirteen 
percent of County revenues remain unrestricted within the General Fund. O’Hare additionally reviewed 
General Fund reserves, contingency levels, and the County’s reliance upon property tax collections and 
Secure Rural Schools funding assumptions. 
 
O’Hare also reviewed several significant departmental budget changes included within the proposed budget, 
including adjustments to staffing levels within Emergency Management, Planning, Finance, Information 
Technology, Legal Counsel, Public Health, Juvenile programs, and Internal Services. Discussion occurred 
regarding reductions in Forestry Reserve expenditures, Public Health restructuring efforts, Fairgrounds 
funding reserves, and capital project adjustments within the Property Reserve Fund. O’Hare stated that more 
detailed information regarding individual departmental budgets would be presented throughout the upcoming 
Budget Committee meetings. 
 
The Budget Committee thanked O’Hare and the Finance Department staff for their work preparing the 
proposed budget documents. Exhibit 1 – Fiscal Year 2026–2027 Budget Message.  
3) Budget Committee establishes meeting calendar 
a. Proposed date: May 21st, 2026, 9:00 a.m. – 12:00 p.m. 
b. Proposed presentations: To be determined by Budget Committee 
The Budget Committee discussed the proposed schedule for future Budget Committee meetings and 
departmental presentations. Arthur O’Hare explained that the proposed presentation schedule included all 
County departments and that the Budget Committee could determine which departments would present and 
whether adjustments to the proposed calendar were necessary. O’Hare stated that, based upon prior budget 
processes, he generally recommended prioritizing presentations from departments that directly interact with 
the public or departments requesting additional staffing or budget adjustments.  
Discussion occurred regarding balancing transparency, public engagement, scheduling limitations, and 
efficient use of the Budget Committee’s time. Commissioner Martin stated that one of the original goals of 
the expanded presentations was to increase transparency and allow the public to better understand County 
operations and services. Additional discussion occurred regarding whether broader departmental 
presentations may be more appropriate through a future “State of the County” style public event outside the 
compressed annual budget timeline.  
Budget Committee members discussed scheduling conflicts and possible revisions to the proposed calendar. 
Dorothy Yetter advised the Committee of a previously scheduled vacation conflict involving certain future 
meeting dates. Marc Kirby expressed interest in hearing presentations from all County departments in order 
to better understand County services and organizational structure. Commissioners additionally discussed 
staffing reductions included within the proposed budget and the importance of allowing departments the 
opportunity to explain operational impacts and staffing requests.  
Further discussion occurred regarding law enforcement compensation, retention concerns involving Sheriff’s 
Office personnel and District Attorney staffing, and potential future compensation studies or collective 
bargaining considerations. O’Hare explained that future compensation adjustments involving represented 
employees would likely require additional review, negotiation, and possible supplemental budget action at a 
later date. Commissioners discussed reserve funding, contingency balances, and long-term sustainability of 
law enforcement funding structures.  
Following discussion, Commissioner Martin made a motion to approve revisions to the proposed Budget 
Committee calendar, including moving the proposed May 26, 2026 meeting to the afternoon of May 27, 
2026, while retaining the elected officials compensation presentation on the May 27 schedule and adjusting 
departmental presentation groupings accordingly. Seconded by Mr. Kirby. Upon roll call, Dorothy Yetter – 
Yes, Colene Martin – Yes, Ron Smith – Yes, Marc Kirby – Yes, and Gary Richardson – Yes. Motion carried 
5-0. 
 
4) Public Comment (limit of three minutes each) 
No public testimony was provided. 
The meeting adjourned at 9:52 a.m.
