2. Department Business 
a. Community Development – Building Safety 
1. Out of State Travel Authorization 
Frank Wharregard, Building Official, and Ron Todd, Deputy Building Official, presented a request for out-
of-state travel authorization for the department’s Commercial Plans Examiner to attend the International Code 
Council (ICC) Annual Business Meeting in Nashville, Tennessee. Mr. Wharregard explained that she serves 
as Secretary-Treasurer of the Southern Oregon Chapter of ICC and President-Elect of the Oregon Permit 
Technicians Association, which is paying her airfare, lodging and registration. Of the $3,675 total cost of the 
trip, the County is asked to cover only $395 in per diem meals from the Building Safety Fund. He noted that 
ICC has asked her to speak on a panel about the career path from permit technician to building official. He 
also explained that Oregon adopts the ICC model codes as the State specialty codes, so participation gives 
Southern Oregon a voice in code development. 
Mr. Wharregard stated that the meeting provides continuing education, networking with peers across the state 
and country, and renewed motivation for staff, and that feedback from local customers can be carried to the 
state organization on behalf of Southern Oregon. The Board noted the history behind building codes, observing 
that codes developed with input from fire officials, manufacturers, designers and contractors have prevented 
tragedies that go uncounted. Mr. Wharregard added that the department’s role is to work with customers to 
comply with State requirements rather than to dictate to them, and that staff would report back to the Board 
after the trip. 
Commissioner Martin moved to approve the out-of-state travel authorization as presented. Commissioner 
Richardson seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - 
Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0.

2. 4-H/Extension and Service District 
a. Grant Application 
Commissioner Richardson presented the 4-H/Extension Service District grant application for agricultural 
education. He explained that the District has $30,000 available for agricultural education grants, that the Public 
Information Officer will advertise the program to interested groups, and that the Board would review the 
applications received and decide which, if any, to award. He envisioned funding three or four projects but 
noted that a single worthy project could receive the full $30,000. Commissioners agreed that eligibility should 
extend to youth organizations such as FFA and 4-H as well as adult agricultural education, and could include 
facility or capital projects tied to agricultural education, and suggested approaching the Farm Bureau as a 
potential partner. 
Commissioner Martin stated that applicants will need clear criteria, including a maximum request amount, 
eligible age groups and project types. She noted that the application prepared by Legal Counsel contains no 
insurance requirement and asked that applicants provide a certificate of insurance naming the County and 
holding it harmless, as was required for the Economic Development grant program, along with a final report 
on accomplishments. Commissioners noted that the application states submission does not create a contract 
and that a contract would be negotiated with any selected applicant, where such terms can also be included. 
The Board agreed to return the application to Legal Counsel to add an insurance clause. 
Commissioner Richardson moved to approve the 4-H/Extension Service District grant application as 
presented, amended to have Legal Counsel add an insurance clause. Commissioner Smith seconded the 
motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - 
Yes. Motion carried 3-0. 
Following the vote, the Board set the application period as November 1 through November 30, 2026, and 
agreed that the program would be announced by press release and posted on the County website, with the 
posting location to be determined with the Public Information Officer. Commissioners noted that the 
Economic Development grant application should carry the same insurance requirement and would be referred 
to the Finance Director.
