1.  Sales Order Acknowledgement; Contract acceptance.
Customer agrees to and accepts the terms of the attached Sales Order Acknowledgment(s) including the seed and seedling
prices and order quantities.  This contract must be electronically signed by Customer and returned to WNR within thirty days
of receipt, along with 50% down payment of the full purchase price for this Contract to be in force and the orders to be
considered valid.  Except as set forth in this Contract, no other terms and conditions apply. This Contract may be amended by
written agreement of both parties and/or the issuance of revised or additional Sales Order Acknowledgement(s).
2.  Seed for Growing Seedings:
Seed for growing seedlings will be provided by Customer, or Customer may elect to purchase seed from WNR at the prices
set forth on Exhibit 1. If Customer provides seed, the following provisions apply:
a.  Customer must deliver the seed stats such as germination, purity and seeds per pound to WNR no later than September
15th.  Third party lab test results are preferred.  Seed must be delivered to WNR no later than October 31st. Fraser and
Canaan seed needs to be delivered by Oct 1st if possible.
b.  If Customer provides seed without germination information, WNR will determine whether a germination test can be
completed in a timely manner.  If WNR determines there is sufficient time, WNR will conduct the test.  If WNR determines
there is insufficient time, WNR will reasonably determine the amount of seed to be used for sowing based on the best
available information and a seed sample will be taken and set aside for later testing if WNR determines that doing so is
warranted. Customer is responsible for all Seed Service Charges set out in Exhibit 1.
c.  If WNR determines that the amount of seed delivered is not sufficient to produce the ordered quantities of seedlings,
WNR will notify Customer and the parties then will either reduce the order amount or arrange for additional seed if time
allows.
d.  If WNR determines that seed provided by Customer does not meet the seed lot standards required by its nurseries, WNR
will notify Customer of any additional seed lot cleaning services needed to bring the seed lot up to minimum standards.
Customer shall either pay for such additional seed cleaning services at the prices set forth on Exhibit 1 or, if time allows,
provide an alternative seed lot. This does not include cone processing.
e.  If Customer-provided seed does not produce the contracted volume of seedlings, Customer shall remain responsible to
pay for the full requested order for that seed lot at the Contract price.
f.   If Customer provided seed does not test >40% germ by WNR's methods, WNR may choose to not accept the seed and
cancel the sow order.

3.  Services to be Provided by WNR.  WNR will:
a.  Cultivate, culture, and provide commercial tree seedlings of the types and estimated quantities described in the Sales
Order Acknowledgment(s).
b.  Maintain Customer's seed and seedlings separate from all other seed and seedlings at its nursery.
c.  Notify Customer of the estimated number of seedlings expected to be available for shipment, and of any unexpected
changes in those estimates.
d.  WNR will use it's commercially reasonable efforts to have the seedlings ready for Customer pickup on the dates and times
requested by Customer as may be agreed to by the Parties ("Pick-up Date"), subject to Section 3.h, below.  Customer's
storage facility preference will be taken into consideration but may need to be changed based on space and trucking.
Customer may only request storage at a nursery if those seedlings are being grown at that facility.
e.  Reserves the right to charge for seedling storage at the rates listed in Exhibit 1 if seedling stock is left more than 10
calendar days after the Pick-up Date.  WNR may agree to store seedlings up to May 1, or later with approval, for spring
shipments if WNR determines that suitable space is available, at the rate listed in Exhibit 1.
f.   Arrange for and fund agricultural inspections needed for shipment.  WNR will bill Customer for any inspection fees at the
prices listed in Exhibit 1.
g.  Load the pallets and/or racks of seedlings on trucks provided by Customer at the Pick-up Location.  Loose loads on
vehicles larger than standard passenger pickup trucks will be assessed the hand loading fee listed in Exhibit 1.
h.  Use reasonable efforts to grow customer's seedlings at the WNR nursery location requested by Customer if WNR
determines that doing is feasible.  WNR will provide trucking to Pick-up Location during the spring lift season of January 1 to
April 30.  If customer requests trucking outside of those dates to a Pick-up Location, customer will be responsible for
payment of shipping.
4. Customer Obligations
a.  If Customer reduces or cancels an order prior to seed stratification, the down payment toward the purchase price will be
refunded, less any costs of seed or other services incurred by WNR.
b.  If Customer reduces or cancels an order after seed stratification, the down payment toward the purchase price becomes
non-refundable and WNR will invoice Customer for costs associated with volumes that WNR is unable to resell, using
commercially reasonable efforts, within thirty days of cancelation.
c.  Customer must provide WNR a written "lift request" at least 3 weeks in advance of the Pick-up Date. All seedlings will be
lifted by April 15 for spring shipments unless WNR determines that weather conditions dictate a different lift date.
d.  If WNR does not receive a lift request by February 1 or communication from Customer regarding stored stock by May 1,
WNR has the unilateral right to dispose of the seedlings as it sees fit and invoice Customer for the disposal costs.
e.  Customer will accept the seedlings FOB Pick-up Location during normal working hours or at other times acceptable to
WNR.
f.  Customer will inspect the seedlings at or immediately after pick-up and promptly notify WNR of any damage to or quality
problems with the seedlings. Any claims must be filed in writing by July 1 of the same year for all spring shipments or by
July 1 of the following year for fall shipments. The validity and resolution of all claims shall be determined by WNR in its sole
discretion.
g.  Customer will return any WNR racks and/or pallets as soon as possible. Any racks and/pallets that are not returned by
June 30 for spring shipments or November 1 for fall shipments will be subject to a charge as listed in Exhibit 1.
5.  Estimated quantity, excess and cull seedlings.
The order quantities listed on the Sales Order Acknowledgment are production targets.  Customer is obligated to pay for only
the quantities actually produced, up to the order amount plus 10%.  In the event of overrun (>10% of order volume),
Customer has the option to purchase the excess seedlings at the contract price. If Customer elects not to purchase the
excess seedlings, they become the property of WNR.  Any seedlings not meeting contract specifications per the Sales Order
Acknowledgment will be considered culls, and Customer has an option to purchase them at a negotiated price "AS IS, WITH
ALL FAULTS" without any warranty or liability by WNR.

normal practices.

9.  Claims, Dispute Resolution.
Any claims that WNR has breached this agreement must be made in writing within 30 days after the alleged breaching event
occurred or first became known to Customer, but in no event later than July 1 of the same year for all spring shipments or by
July 1 of the following year for fall shipments. The validity and resolution of all claims shall be determined by WNR in its sole

objections specifying different or additional facts and the basis for them.  If the parties continue to disagree, they will
negotiate a joint statement of the facts that are agreed on, and the additional facts asserted by each of them but
contested by the other.  As soon as practicable after that, officers or officials of each party who have settlement authority will
meet and make good faith efforts to reach agreement on a fair and appropriate solution to the dispute. Regardless, Customer
agrees and understands that WNR's aggregate liability for any and all claims, losses, costs, damages of any nature
