Legal Counsel Update 
1. Declaration of Potential Conflicts of Interest 
Commissioners Richardson, Martin, and Smith each declared no potential conflicts of interest. 
Chair Ron Smith called for Executive Session at 9:31 a.m. pursuant to ORS 192.660(2)(h), to consult with 
counsel concerning the legal rights and duties of the County regarding current litigation or litigation likely to be 
filed. Accredited news-media representatives were directed not to report or disclose the deliberations or 
statements made during Executive Session. Chair Smith stated that no final decision would be made in 
Executive Session and that the Board would return to Open Session before taking action. 
Additional Attendees: Helene Lulich, Property Manager; Chrissy Ewald, Daily Courier; and Cliff Davidson 
(remote).

Legal Counsel Update Continued 
3. Gabbert et al v. Josephine County et al; USDC 1:23-cv-01434-CL - Surplus Litigation 
potential settlement 
The Board reconvened in Open Session at 10:28 a.m. Chair Smith stated that the Board had received an 
update in Executive Session and had directed counsel regarding next steps in Gabbert et al. v. Josephine 
County et al. No further action was taken at that time. 
4. Amendment to Resolution No. 2026-031; SEL801; Renewal of Adult Jail and Juvenile 
Detention Five Year Local Option Tax 
Tabled. No action was taken. 
5. Public Comment - US Forest Service - Proposed rule to rescind the 2001 Roadless Area 
Conservation Rule 
Commissioner Smith discussed a draft County comment letter concerning the U.S. Forest Service proposal 
to rescind the 2001 Roadless Area Conservation Rule. He stated that rescission could affect access to and 
potential economic use of federal lands in Josephine County, including mining and other resource-related 
activities. County Counsel Wally Hicks had reviewed the draft and had no proposed changes. Commissioner 
Smith stated that he would circulate the draft to Commissioners Richardson and Martin for review before 
submitting the County's comments within the federal comment period. No formal vote was taken. 
6. Additional Subjects/Matters from Commissioners - ORS 192.640(1) 
Commissioners reported no additional matters for Board consideration. 
There being no further business, the Legal Counsel Update adjourned at 10:31 a.m.
