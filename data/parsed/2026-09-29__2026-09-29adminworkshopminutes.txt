2. Department Business
a.
Forestry
1. Seedling Growing Service Contract 2027A-114 with Weyerhaeuser NR Company for
reforestation of Josephine County lands – $120,280 – Approve contract and
authorize Dave Streeter to electronically sign
Dave Streeter, Forestry Director, requested approval of Seedling Growing Service Contract 2027A-114 with 
Weyerhaeuser NR Company, covering approximately 190,000 seedlings for future reforestation of Josephine 
County lands. He described the informal solicitation of nurseries and evaluation of seedling quality and value, 
explaining that the County uses both one-year container-grown plugs and two-year bare-root stock. Ordering 
in advance of anticipated timber harvests helps the County replant promptly and avoid the cost of replacing 
lower-quality stock. The multi-year contract totals $120,280. 
Commissioner Martin moved to approve Seedling Growing Service Contract 2027A-114 with Weyerhaeuser 
NR Company for $120,280 and authorize Dave Streeter to sign electronically. Commissioner Richardson 
seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; 
Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0. 
b. Recreation
1. CXT Upper Loop Restroom Purchase for the newly renovated loop at Whitehorse
Park – State of Oregon Contract #1542 – $146,883
Tamra Martin, Recreation Director, proposed purchasing a modular CXT Dakota restroom to serve the 
renovated upper camping loop at Whitehorse Park, where the existing lower restroom is a considerable 
distance away. She explained that the $146,883 purchase was planned with ARPA funding, but she had just 
learned the installation, not merely the purchase, might have to be completed by December 31, 2026. The 
original installation was planned for March 2027. She was confirming whether the vendor could deliver and 
install the unit within the earlier deadline; County staff could prepare the site. If the timeline or ARPA 
funding could not be secured, she expected to place the project on hold and reconsider funding and timing, 
including potential capital-reserve funds. She requested conditional signing authority to proceed promptly 
only if the funding and schedule proved workable. 
Commissioner Martin moved to authorize Tamra Martin to sign the purchase documents for the CXT 
restroom at Whitehorse Park under State of Oregon Contract No. 1542 for $146,883, provided the project 
qualifies for the intended funding and can meet the required completion timeline. Commissioner Richardson 
seconded the motion. The Board requested that Ms. Martin keep it informed. Upon roll call: Commissioner 
Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0.

c.
Transit
1. Amendment Number 1 to ODOT Grant Agreement No. 35902 – Change the
Statement of Work from “replacement vehicles” to “expansion vehicles” – $288,000
grant with $72,000 match – Approval and signature
Scott Chancey, Transit Program Supervisor, presented Amendment No. 1 to ODOT Grant Agreement No. 
35902. ODOT had inadvertently used the statement of work for replacement vehicles on a grant intended to 
purchase two additional, or expansion, vehicles. Mr. Chancey explained that the error duplicated vehicle 
identification numbers appearing on a separate replacement-vehicle grant. The amendment corrects the 
classification without changing the $288,000 grant or $72,000 local match. ODOT required the signed 
correction before Transit could place the vehicle order. Mr. Chancey noted that the County was coordinating 
orders across several grants to obtain purchase efficiencies and that vehicle delivery can take approximately 
six to ten months. 
Commissioner Martin moved to approve Amendment No. 1 to ODOT Grant Agreement No. 35902 for the 
Board’s signature so Transit could proceed with its vehicle purchase. Commissioner Richardson seconded 
the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner 
Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0. 
d. Planning
1. Findings of Fact and Decision – Appeal of a Director’s Decision to Approve a
Processing Facility at 2581 Dick George Road, Cave Junction (Appellant: Joe &
Bonnie Campanoli; Applicant: Columbia Wholesale LLC) – Approved by the Board
on August 31, 2026
James Black, Planning and Terri Woodruff, Associate Planner, presented the Findings of Fact and Decision 
arising from the appeal by Joe and Bonnie Campanoli concerning Columbia Wholesale LLC’s proposed 
processing facility at 2581 Dick George Road, Cave Junction. The Board had heard the appeal on August 24 
and continued it on August 31, when it affirmed the approval subject to additional conditions. Staff reviewed 
the conditions reflected in Exhibit B, including no water use in processing, access for processing activities 
from Dick George Road rather than Greenview Road, and carbon filtration for odor control. Following 
approval of the written findings, staff would issue and mail the notice of decision to participants; a 21-day 
appeal period would precede further permitting if no appeal were filed. 
Commissioner Richardson moved to approve the Findings of Fact and Decision for the appeal of the 
Director’s approval of the processing facility at 2581 Dick George Road, including the conditions presented 
in Exhibit B. Commissioner Martin seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; 
Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0.

c.
Sheriff’s Office Budgeted Positions
Commissioner Richardson discussed six budgeted Sheriff’s Office positions that would remain
unfilled due to budget constraints: two Patrol Deputy, two Corrections Deputy, and two Major
Crimes Detective positions. He explained that the reductions had been discussed during the
supplemental-budget process, but Finance had subsequently finalized the corresponding FTE
adjustments following the negotiated collective bargaining agreements. The Board considered
also presenting the matter at a Weekly Business Session for public awareness, while noting
agenda-posting time and confirming coordination with the Sheriff’s Office.
Commissioner Richardson moved to adjust the Sheriff’s Office budgeted FTE by removing the six
identified unfilled positions. Commissioner Martin seconded the motion. Upon roll call:
Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion
carried 3-0. The Board discussed bringing the matter to a future Weekly Business Session for
additional public awareness.
4. OTHER
The Board discussed a proposed Outstanding Resource Waters designation affecting Illinois Valley
watersheds. Commissioners raised questions about possible implications for Cave Junction, industrial
property, federal-land management, wildfire mitigation, and invasive-weed control. They also discussed
seasonal low water flows and high temperatures in the Illinois River. Commissioner Richardson stated
that he was gathering additional information and might return with a proposed resolution for
consideration during the coming legislative session. No formal action was taken.
5. ADDITIONAL SUBJECT MATTERS FROM COMMISSIONERS/ANNOUNCEMENTS:
During announcements, the Board noted that fire season was nearing its end but residents should continue to 
exercise caution until substantial rain arrives. Chair Smith adjourned the meeting at 1:59 p.m.
