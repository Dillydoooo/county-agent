BEFORE THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS FOR JOSEPHINE COUNTY 
 
STATE OF OREGON 
 
 
 
In the Matter of Temporary Delegation of      ) 
Signing Authority During the Absence of 
) 
ORDER No. 2026-054 
the Josephine County Finance Director 
) 
 
 
WHEREAS, the Josephine County Finance Director is responsible for the administration 
and oversight of the financial operations of Josephine County; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Josephine County Finance Director will be temporarily absent from 
County operations beginning September 28, 2026 through October 23, 2026; and 
 
WHEREAS, during the temporary absence of the Finance Director, it is necessary to 
provide for continuity of County financial operations and the timely review, approval, and 
execution of documents ordinarily requiring the signature or approval of the Finance Director; and 
 
WHEREAS, Trevin Mikels, Assistant Finance Director, possesses the knowledge and 
experience necessary to provide such continuity during the Finance Director's temporary 
absence; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners has determined that it is in the best 
interest of Josephine County to temporarily delegate signing authority to Trevin Mikels, 
Assistant Finance Director, for the limited period of the Finance Director's absence. 
 
NOW, THEREFORE, based upon the foregoing and good cause appearing, IT IS 
HEREBY ORDERED that Trevin Mikels, Assistant Finance Director, is temporarily authorized 
to review, approve, and execute documents on behalf of the Josephine County Finance 
Department that would ordinarily require the signature or approval of the Finance Director, subject 
to all applicable County policies, adopted budget authority, ordinances, and state and federal law. 
 
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the authority granted by this Order is limited to those 
actions necessary to maintain the ordinary and ongoing operations of the Josephine County 
Finance Department and shall not exceed the authority otherwise vested in the Finance Director. 
 
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that this temporary delegation does not constitute an 
appointment as Finance Director or Budget Officer and does not transfer any authority that, by 
law or prior Order of the Board, must be separately designated or exercised by the Board of 
County Commissioners. 
 
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that the authority granted herein shall be effective beginning 
September 28, 2026 and shall automatically terminate at the close of business on October 23, 
2026, or upon the Finance Director's return to duty, whichever occurs first, unless otherwise 
extended or modified by further action of the Board of County Commissioners.
