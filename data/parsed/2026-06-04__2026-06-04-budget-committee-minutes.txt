3) Department Presentations: 
a. Public Health – Janet Fredrickson Exhibit 1 PowerPoint Presentation 
Janet Fredrickson, Public Health Administrator and Project Manager Michael DeRosset presented the 
proposed Public Health budget and provided an overview of the department's diverse programs and services. 
Fredrickson reviewed responsibilities including Environmental Health, Communicable Disease, Clinical 
Services, Reproductive Health, Immunizations, WIC, Vital Records, Animal Shelter oversight, Jail Medical 
Services, Air Quality, Public Health Emergency Preparedness, and newly returned Solid Waste 
responsibilities. 
Discussion focused on staffing challenges experienced during the previous year, including the loss of multiple 
positions and the department's efforts to maintain services despite reduced staffing levels. Fredrickson 
highlighted ongoing outreach efforts, communicable disease prevention, public health education, 
environmental health inspections, vaccination programs, and increasing demand for services throughout 
Josephine County. Discussion also occurred regarding the Public Health Emergency Preparedness Coordinator 
position, grant-funded programs, telehealth initiatives, WIC services, and future operational goals. Committee 
members discussed staffing needs, solid waste enforcement responsibilities, grant opportunities, and long-term 
sustainability of public health services. –  Exhibit 1 PowerPoint Presentation 
 
b. Animal Shelter- Laura Jansen Presentation  
Laura Jansen, Animal Shelter Manager, presented the proposed Animal Shelter budget and reviewed shelter 
operations, animal control services, and public health responsibilities associated with animal welfare and 
rabies prevention. Jansen explained that the shelter provides care for animals impounded through law 
enforcement actions, animal control investigations, neglect and abuse cases, dangerous animal proceedings, 
quarantine requirements, and owner surrenders. The shelter additionally provides adoption and reunification 
services intended to safely return animals to their owners or place them into appropriate homes. Discussions 
occurred regarding rabies prevention, dangerous dog investigations, animal licensing, public safety concerns, 
shelter capacity, veterinary care, and efforts to reduce pet overpopulation. Committee members discussed 
operational demands, adoption services, animal control responsibilities, and the shelter's role in supporting 
both public safety and public health objectives throughout Josephine County – Exhibit 2 PowerPoint 
Presentation 
 
4) Budget Officer answers questions/provides requested information 
Budget Officer Arthur O'Hare responded to questions raised throughout departmental presentations and 
provided clarification regarding staffing allocations, grant funding, internal service fund charges, reserve 
accounts, operational expenditures, and long-term budget planning considerations. Discussion occurred 
regarding future funding sustainability, departmental staffing requests, and fiscal impacts associated with 
proposed budget amendments.

6) Budget Committee Deliberation 
Following completion of departmental presentations and public comment, the Budget Committee entered 
deliberations regarding proposed amendments to the Fiscal Year 2026-2027 Budget. Committee members 
reviewed staffing requests, departmental operational needs, reserve levels, long-term sustainability concerns, 
and recommendations previously submitted by departments and elected officials. Discussion occurred 
regarding balancing service demands with available resources while maintaining appropriate reserve levels 
for future fiscal stability.

• General Fund (Fund 80) and Building Reserve fund (Fund 81)

and office staff, supplies and travel.
• OSU to assign and fund faculty to serve Josephine County and provide central 
services to support county operations and program delivery.
• County to reimburse OSU quarterly for actual expenses based on 
approved budget.
