Standing attachment. The department proposes its five figures with its first report. Definitions change only by Board 
action and are footnoted for one year after a change. Where a figure cannot yet be produced, record the source that will 
produce it and the period it begins.

Overtime definition and flag rule, county-wide (applies to the overtime line on page 1, the overtime column on page 3, and 
any overtime key figure): overtime is hours paid at premium rate plus compensatory time earned at premium rate in the 
period, taken from the timekeeping and payroll systems (Administrative Policy D-04) by pay periods ending in the period. 
Report it as a percent of regular hours paid and as dollars as a percent of personnel services actual. Grant- or event-reimbursed 
overtime is shown separately. Aggregates only; no individual data in the packet. On page 3, coverage of a function by one 
person working overtime is recorded as a gap, not a backup. Finance flags overtime that exceeds the adopted overtime budget 
by 10 percent or the same period last year by 20 percent; the department explains cause, action and date back on plan on page 
1. A department above 5 percent of regular hours or $100,000 a year carries overtime as one of its five key figures.
