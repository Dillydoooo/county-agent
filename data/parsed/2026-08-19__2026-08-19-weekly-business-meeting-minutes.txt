3. PUBLIC HEARING: First Reading of Ordinance No. 2026-002; AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE 
COMPREHENSIVE PLAN OF JOSEPHINE COUNTY (ORDINANCE 81-11 AS AMENDED) FROM FARM 
TO RESIDENTIAL AND AMEND THE ZONING MAP OF JOSEPHINE COUNTY (ORDINANCE 85-1 AS 
AMENDED) FROM FARM RESOURCE (FR) TO RURAL RESIDENTIAL (RR5) AND GOAL 
EXCEPTION. THE SUBJECT PROPERTY IS IDENTIFIED IN THE JOSEPHINE COUNTY ASSESSOR'S 
RECORDS AS MAP 35-06-03-D0, TAX LOT (TL) 1400. THE PROPERTY OWNER IS ROSE BUTTE LLC.: 
Chair Smith opened the Public Hearing at 9:00 a.m. 
Commissioner Richardson made a motion to read Ordinance No. 2026-002 by title only. Commissioner Martin 
seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner 
Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0. 
Tami Smith, Senior Planner, presented the staff report. She stated that the Josephine County Rural Planning 
Commission held a public hearing on October 27, 2025, and recommended approval. The Board of County 
Commissioners subsequently held a public hearing on March 9, 2026, approved the application, and adopted the 
matter on April 8, 2026. No appeals were filed. Staff recommended approval of the first reading and scheduling the 
second reading for September 2, 2026. 
The Board had no questions for staff. Chair Smith invited testimony on the ordinance; none was offered. 
Commissioner Richardson made a motion to approve the first reading of 
Ordinance No. 2026-002 and schedule the 
second reading for September 2, 2026. Commissioner Martin seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner 
Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried Yes-0. 
Chair Smith closed the public hearing at 9:04 a.m.
