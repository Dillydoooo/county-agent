Legal Counsel Update 
1. Declaration of Potential Conflicts of Interest 
Commissioners Richardson, Martin, and Smith each declared no potential conflicts of 
interest regarding the matters before the Board. 
2. RFP for Contracted Attorney Services 
Wally Hicks, Legal Counsel, reported that the County issued the RFP for contracted attorney 
services and received a response from Christian Boenisch, Esq., former Yamhill County 
Counsel. Mr. Boenisch summarized his experience, including approximately 13 years as 
Yamhill County Counsel, prior service as a deputy county counsel, private practice, in-house 
counsel work, and more than seven years with the Oregon Department of Justice. The Board 
discussed the anticipated relationship between outside counsel, County Legal Counsel, and 
the Board, including the need for outside counsel to exercise professional discretion when 
representing the County and Board. Mr. Hicks recommended a one-year personal services 
contract beginning at approximately 60 hours per month. Mr. Boenisch's proposal identified 
an hourly range of $175 to $225; the Board discussed using the County's established 
government attorney rate while separately addressing travel time. Mr. Hicks stated the 
contract would be paid from the Legal Counsel budget and would cost less than filling another 
staff attorney position. Without objection, the Board authorized Mr. Hicks to negotiate and 
enter into the personal services contract with Mr. Boenisch substantially on those terms. 
3. Resolution No. 2026-039; In the Matter of Affirming Compliance with State and 
Federal Law and Requesting That the State of Oregon Address Counties’ Federal 
Funding and False Claims Act Exposure 
Wally Hicks, Legal Counsel, presented Resolution No. 2026-039 and reviewed the Board's 
prior discussions concerning potential County exposure when state and federal legal 
requirements conflict with conditions attached to federal funding. The Board reviewed the 
final draft and agreed to amend paragraph C of the resolved section to include County "officers 
and employees" among those for whom the State would be asked to provide defense and 
indemnification against False Claims Act exposure. The Board noted that the proposed 
resolution had previously been discussed at Weekly Business Sessions and had been publicly 
available for comment. Commissioner Richardson moved to approve Resolution No. 2026-
039, In the Matter of Affirming Compliance with State and Federal Law and Requesting That 
the State of Oregon Address Counties' Federal Funding and False Claims Act Exposure, as 
amended to include "officers and employees." Commissioner Martin seconded the motion. 
Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner 
Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0.
