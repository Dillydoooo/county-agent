APPROVED ON NOVEMBER 4, 2026 
BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
AT THE WEEKLY BUSINESS SESSION 
Open Session: October 30, 2026 
2:30 p.m. - Board of Commissioners Conference Room 
Attending: Commissioners Ron A. Smith; Colene M. Martin; Gary L. Richardson; and Nikole Guzman, Recorder 
Chair Ron Smith called the meeting to order at 2:30 p.m. 
1. OPEN SESSION 
a. To review and evaluate the employment-related performance of the chief executive officer of any public 
body, a public officer, employee or staff member who requests an open hearing. 
Natalie Eckhardt-Doupe, Interim Human Resources Director, and Ryan Johnson, Facilities Director, joined the Board. 
Because Mr. Johnson had requested an open hearing, the Board proceeded in Open Session to address the employment-
related performance matter. 
Ms. Eckhardt-Doupe presented Mr. Johnson with the Performance Improvement Plan and the informal investigation report 
resulting from the Board's prior review and the October 28, 2026 open meeting. She explained that the Performance 
Improvement Plan was intended to establish expectations and begin implementation that day, and that an employee signature 
would acknowledge receipt and discussion of the plan rather than agreement with its contents. 
Mr. Johnson read a statement objecting to the process and to the Board's decision to place him on a Performance 
Improvement Plan. He stated that he had not received the documents before the meeting, disputed the process and several 
of the performance concerns, requested that the matter be tabled to allow time for review with legal counsel, and requested 
that the matter be removed from his employment record. Mr. Johnson stated that he was not feeling well and intended to 
take time away from work, then left the meeting without signing the Performance Improvement Plan. 
Ms. Eckhardt-Doupe advised that Mr. Johnson had submitted a formal appeal on October 30. She reviewed the appeal 
procedure under County personnel policy, including the opportunity to submit supporting materials within 14 days and the 
ability to appeal an initial determination to the Board of County Commissioners. She further advised that the appeal process 
was separate from the Performance Improvement Plan and that the plan could take effect that day. 
The Board discussed the investigation process and asked questions regarding the informal investigation, the contents of the 
report, and whether information received from other elected officials had been considered. Ms. Eckhardt-Doupe explained 
that the matter had proceeded as an informal investigation, that Mr. Johnson had been interviewed by the Board on October 
28, and that the resulting Performance Improvement Plan was a non-disciplinary tool intended to identify expectations and 
areas requiring improvement. She stated that employment counsel had reviewed the plan. 
Commissioner Richardson questioned portions of the report in light of information provided by the County Clerk and 
County Treasurer. Ms. Eckhardt-Doupe explained how those matters had been addressed in the final report and stated that 
the remaining findings reflected the information considered by the Board during the process. Commissioner Martin asked 
about Mr. Johnson's statement that he intended to take leave; Ms. Eckhardt-Doupe stated that Human Resources would 
contact him for clarification. 
Chair Smith left the meeting before its conclusion due to a personal emergency. Vice-Chair Martin assumed the chair. Ms. 
Eckhardt-Doupe clarified that copies of the Performance Improvement Plan had been provided to all Commissioners earlier 
in the week. The Board discussed the next procedural steps. No formal motion or roll-call vote was taken. 
2. EXECUTIVE SESSION 
a. To review and evaluate the employment-related performance of the chief executive officer of any public 
body, a public officer, employee or staff member who does not request an open hearing - ORS 
192.660(2)(i). 
Not convened. Mr. Johnson requested an open hearing, and the Board therefore continued the matter in Open Session. 
3. OPEN SESSION CON’D 
a. To review and evaluate the employment-related performance of the chief executive officer of any public 
body, a public officer, employee or staff member who requests an open hearing. 
The Executive Session was not convened. Because Mr. Johnson requested an open hearing, the Board remained in Open 
Session throughout the meeting. No formal action was taken. 
Vice-Chair Martin adjourned the meeting at 2:47 p.m.
