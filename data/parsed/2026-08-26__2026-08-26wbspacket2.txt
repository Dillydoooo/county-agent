        Resolution No. 2026-032
WHEREAS, the State of Oregon is accepting applications for grants from the Industrial 
Site Loan Fund (ISLF) and the Regional Infrastructure Fund (RIF); and
WHEREAS, Josephine County desires to participate in this grant program to the greatest 
extent possible as a means of providing necessary improvements; and
WHEREAS, the Board of Josephine County Commissioners have identified the 
extension of water and sewer lines outside the City of Cave Junction as a high-priority need; 
and 
WHEREAS, project details include extending water and sewer lines to the 
unincorporated community of Kerby, approximately 3 miles north of Cave Junction, and to the 
Illinois Valley Airport, approximately 3 miles south of Cave Junction; now, therefore
IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED that the Board of County Commissioners for Josephine 
County demonstrates its support for the County’s submittal of its application to receive grant 
funds from the ISLF.
IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED that Josephine County hereby pledges its full faith and 
credit in support of the County’s grant application.
IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED that Josephine County is a committed partner of the City of 
Cave Junction in this project, and the County fully supports the City’s application for a grant 
from the RIF for the same project.
IT IS FURTHER RESOLVED that if both grants are awarded, the County and City will 
either decline one of the grants or split the scope of each grant to ensure that the same cost will 
never be assigned to both grants.

Resolution No. 2026-032
Item 3A
Re: Letter of Support – City of Cave Junction Water and Sewer Line 
Extension Project
To Whom It May Concern:
The Josephine County Board of Commissioners is pleased to express its 
strong support for the City of Cave Junction’s application for funding through 
the Regional Infrastructure Fund to assist with the proposed water and sewer 
line extension project.
Reliable water and wastewater infrastructure is essential to the health, 
safety, economic vitality, and long-term sustainability of our communities. 
The proposed extension will help address critical infrastructure needs in the 
Illinois Valley and provide capacity to support responsible growth and 
development.
Josephine County recognizes the importance of investing in infrastructure 
that strengthens local communities and creates opportunities for future 
economic development. Improvements to the City’s water and sewer 
systems will benefit existing residents and businesses while helping position 
Cave Junction to accommodate future housing, commercial activity, and 
other community needs.
This project also represents an important investment in the broader Illinois 
Valley and throughout Josephine County. Strong municipal infrastructure 
contributes to a more resilient local economy, supports public health and 
environmental protection, and enhances the ability of communities to attract 
and retain businesses and residents.
For these reasons, the Josephine County Board of Commissioners respectfully 
encourages the State of Oregon to give favorable consideration to the City of 
Cave Junction’s funding application. We believe this project is a worthwhile 
investment in the community and in the future of both the City of Cave 
Junction and Josephine County.
Thank you for your consideration and for your continued investment in 
infrastructure projects that strengthen Oregon communities.
Sincerely,
Josephine County Board of Commissioners

 Order No. 2026-046
Josephine County Budget Officer     )
  (Rescinds and Replaces Order No. 2026-027)
WHEREAS, ORS 294.331 provides the designation of a Budget Officer to prepare and supervise 
the budget document; and
WHEREAS, by Order No. 2026-027 dated May 14, 2026, the Josephine County Board of 
Commissioners designated Arthur O’Hare to serve as the Budget Officer for Josephine 
County; and
WHEREAS, Matthew Eide, Finance Director for Josephine County, Oregon, has access to and 
knowledge of the appropriate department budgets and the accounting records of department 
expenditures; and
WHEREAS, the Board of County Commissioners has determined that Matthew Eide should 
be designated as the Budget Officer for Josephine County.
NOW, THEREFORE, based upon the foregoing and good cause appearing, it is hereby ordered 
that Matthew Eide, Finance Director for Josephine County, Oregon, shall be designated to serve 
Josephine County as the Budget Officer to oversee, prepare or supervise the preparation of the 
budget documents for the Josephine County budget to include all county districts, and shall act 
under the direction of the Board of County Commissioner until terminated by further order of the 
Board.
