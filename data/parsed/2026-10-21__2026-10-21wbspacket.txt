WHEREAS, the Petition was accompanied by:
(1) A certification from the Josephine County Assessor that the petition is signed by the requisite number of 
qualified landowners of the land proposed to be annexed to the district, as required by ORS 198.765.
(2) A written endorsement of approval by the District Board.
(3) A description of the land to be annexed to the district.
