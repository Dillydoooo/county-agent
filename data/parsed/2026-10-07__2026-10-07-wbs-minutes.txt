3. ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION(S) IN CONSIDERATION OF: 
a. Administrative Policy Revision: F-01; Social Media Policy 
Thomas Gray, Public Information Coordinator and Records Management Officer, presented the proposed revisions to 
Administrative Policy F-01. He explained that the County reviewed the policy with Legal Counsel to reflect current law 
and evolving First Amendment considerations involving government social-media accounts. The revisions clarify 
acceptable moderation of comments involving spam, harassment, or other content outside the established rules while 
preserving protected speech, and distinguish responsibilities for accounts operated by elected officials from those operated 
by County departments. 
b. Administrative Policy Revision: F-06; Public Records Policy 
Mr. Gray presented the proposed revisions to Administrative Policy F-06, Public Records Policy. He stated that the 
existing policy required updates to better align with current Oregon public-records law and County practice. The County 
consulted Oregon Public Records Advocate Todd Albert, whose recommendations were incorporated to improve public 
access, consistency, and organization in the records-request process. 
c. Resolution No. 2026-xxx; In the Matter of Affirming Compliance with State and Federal Law and 
Requesting That the State of Oregon Address Counties' Federal Funding and False Claims Act 
Exposure 
Wally Hicks, Legal Counsel, presented a draft resolution concerning County compliance with state and federal law and 
potential exposure under the federal False Claims Act. He explained that the draft was being presented for discussion and 
public input rather than final action and had not yet been assigned a resolution number. The discussion focused on the 
County's obligations when accepting federal funds and the potential difficulty created when federal requirements conflict 
with State of Oregon requirements. Commissioners expressed concern regarding immigration-related federal requirements 
and Oregon sanctuary-state law and discussed asking the State to provide guidance or protection for counties. The Board 
indicated the draft would return for further consideration after additional review and public input. 
4. REQUESTS/COMMENTS FROM CITIZENS: (Each person will be given three (3) minutes to speak) 
Stephen Fitch, Josephine County resident, thanked the Board for assistance in obtaining records he had requested and 
stated that he received the records after approximately ten months. He expressed concerns regarding the length of time 
required to obtain the records, government transparency, due process, and what he characterized as retaliation against 
citizens who exercise constitutional rights. Mr. Fitch asked the Board to continue emphasizing accountability and timely 
access to public information. 
Judy Ahrens, Josephine County resident, spoke regarding public education, academic fundamentals, patriotism, parental 
involvement, and school funding requirements. She encouraged citizens to attend local school-board meetings, including an 
upcoming Three Rivers School District meeting, and to advocate for reading, writing, mathematics, and civic education.

5. BOARD MAY REVIEW PUBLIC COMMENTS/QUESTIONS: 
Commissioner Richardson stated that he was pleased the public-records issue described by Mr. Fitch had moved forward 
and said the County had been working to improve its records-request processes. Regarding education, he discussed District 
7's emphasis on reading and noted that public schools must also meet a variety of state and federal program requirements 
that affect budgeting and priorities. 
Commissioner Martin stated that public officials should not use their positions to discriminate or retaliate against citizens 
and emphasized the Board's responsibility to serve the public and provide clear information. She thanked Mr. Gray for his 
work on public-records improvements and thanked Ms. Ahrens for her continued advocacy regarding education. 
Chair Smith stated that the revisions to the public-records policy reflected the County's effort to listen to public concerns and 
improve access to information. He also thanked Ms. Ahrens for her continued interest in education and civic involvement. 
Commissioner Martin moved to approve Administrative Policy Revision F-01, Social Media Policy. Commissioner Richardson 
seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. 
Motion carried 3-0. 
Commissioner Martin moved to approve Administrative Policy Revision F-06, Public Records Policy. Commissioner Richardson 
seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; Commissioner Smith - Yes. 
Motion carried 3-0.
