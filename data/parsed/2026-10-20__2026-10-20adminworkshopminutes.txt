b. Treasurer 
1. Board Order No. 2026-060 – In the Matter of Temporarily Adopting Oregon State 
Treasury Policies Regarding the Protection of Public Funds and Third Party 
Vendors – Temporary adoption of OST Policies FIN 214 and FIN 216 for 
compliance with ORS Chapter 295 pending adoption of County-specific policies – 
Approval and signature 
Dustin Calvo, Chief Deputy Treasurer, presented Board Order No. 2026-060. He explained that ORS Chapter 
295 requires protections for public funds that are not deposited in a County account at a qualified depository, 
and that funds collected by third-party payment vendors are held for two to three days before transfer to the 
County in accounts that are neither segregated nor collateralized as the statute requires. The statute identifies 
a line of credit as an alternative method of collateralization, the Oregon State Treasury’s policies also allow a 
cash deposit or surety bond, and the Order permits other viable methods to be brought to the Board. The Order 
temporarily adopts OST Policies FIN 214 and FIN 216 as the County’s policies, as ORS 295.098(2) permits, 
until a County-specific policy is developed, which Mr. Calvo estimated would take about six months with 
Legal Counsel’s assistance. 
In response to Commissioner Richardson’s suggestion that the Order include a deadline for returning with the 
permanent policy, Mr. Calvo stated that his immediate priority is bringing the County’s payment vendors, 
principally Point and Pay, into compliance, and that the Order gives the Treasurer authority to require 
collateralization or discontinue a vendor. Commissioner Richardson cautioned that the Order itself does not

Jamie Davis, Regional Director – Southern Region, Oregon State University Extension Service, presented the

b. Proposed revisions to Administrative Policy A-01 (County Policies) and repeal and 
replacement of Administrative Policy A-02 (Leadership Committee) with a Department 
Head Review of Proposed Policies process – Commissioner Richardson 
Commissioner Richardson presented his proposed revisions to Administrative Policy A-01 and the repeal and 
replacement of Administrative Policy A-02, which would replace the Leadership Committee with a written 
Department Head review of proposed policies. He explained that County Counsel had advised the Leadership 
Committee is a relatively recent creation, that in his attendance at its meetings it has not proved as effective 
as other methods, and that the proposal would convert it into leadership reports of the kind discussed under 
Department Reviews. He also questioned the current structure, under which only elected officials – who do 
not report to the Board – hold voting rights on a committee composed largely of appointed department heads. 
Commissioners also discussed the County’s core values and the organizational maturity model as frameworks 
for building the leadership team. 
Chair Smith stated that the committee had not met for about two years until he asked that it be reinstituted, 
that the policy makes the Board responsible for coordinating its meetings and the Board had not kept it on 
task, and that its stated purposes – advising the Board on personnel rules and policies and serving as labor-
management consultants – remain valuable. He did not want department heads to read the proposal as a 
message that their input is unwanted and asked that leadership be surveyed before the policy is changed. 
Commissioner Martin stated that she has attended only one meeting briefly and wanted to sit through a full 
meeting and take a deeper look before assessing the proposal. Commissioners agreed the discussion would 
continue: Commissioner Richardson will bring a revised proposal back to the Board, Chair Smith will research 
the committee’s history and seek department-head feedback with the Public Information Coordinator’s help, 
and Commissioner Martin will return with her thoughts. No formal action was taken. 
c. Final Plat for Nolan Subdivision – Approval and signature 
The final plat for the Nolan Subdivision, located in the Illinois Valley, came before the Board without a staff 
presentation. Commissioner Martin stated that she had reviewed the plat but would have preferred a brief staff 
summary, noting that although the Board’s approval is largely a formality after review by the Planning and 
Surveyor’s Offices, a short summary would let the public understand the housing projects the Board approves, 
which have come at roughly one a month. Chair Smith asked that the Surveyor’s Office also provide a 
conventional location map with each plat rather than only township and range references. Commissioners 
agreed the plat should not be delayed and that, going forward, the Surveyor’s Office should supply a one-page 
executive summary and map with each final plat, which can also be shared with the public through the Public 
Information Coordinator’s weekly update. 
Commissioner Richardson moved to approve the final plat for the Nolan Subdivision and authorize the 
Board to sign, with an executive summary of the plat to be provided by the Surveyor’s Office at the next 
Weekly Business Session and to serve as the template for all future final plats. Commissioner Smith 
seconded the motion. Upon roll call: Commissioner Richardson - Yes; Commissioner Martin - Yes; 
Commissioner Smith - Yes. Motion carried 3-0.
