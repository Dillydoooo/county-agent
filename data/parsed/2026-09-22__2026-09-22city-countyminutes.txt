APPROVED ON SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 
BY THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS 
AT THE WEEKLY BUSINESS SESSION 
 
City/County Monthly Update: September 22, 2026 
11:00 a.m. - Board of Commissioners Conference Room 
Attending: Commissioners Ron A. Smith; Colene M. Martin; Gary L. Richardson; and Nikole Guzman, 
Recorder 
Additional Attendees: Cliff Scherf, Mayor; Aaron Cubic, City Manager; and Rick Riker, Council Member 
Chair Ron Smith called the joint meeting to order at 11:00 a.m. 
1. Public Lands 
The City and County discussed reviewing publicly owned properties located within each other’s 
jurisdictions to identify possible future property exchanges or joint-use opportunities. Discussion focused on 
whether existing downtown parking areas could support a multi-level parking structure as a joint City-
County project. Potential locations, including the Growers Market/Grasshopper area and County-owned 
parking near the courthouse and library, were discussed. The City agreed to bring back its most recent 
downtown parking study, including historical and projected parking demand, so the conversation can 
continue. No decisions were made. 
2. Car Camping in UGB or on County Property 
The City discussed the continuing issue of people living in vehicles and whether a managed overnight 
vehicle-camping area could be developed as a joint City-County effort. The discussion included the City’s 
existing camping regulations, HB 3115, potential locations near the city limits, law-enforcement access, 
liability, insurance, sanitation, security and the possible use of a nonprofit operator under a lease. 
Participants discussed examples from other communities and emphasized that no specific site or operating 
model has been selected. The matter will remain on the agenda for further discussion. 
No decisions were made. 
3. Youth Community Center 
Mayor Scherf raised the idea of creating a youth community center and asked whether a fairgrounds 
building might be considered for year-round use. The group discussed the need for youth recreational and 
community space, existing and planned services through organizations such as the Boys & Girls Club and 
YMCA, the seasonal use of fairgrounds buildings, possible alternate locations, and the long-term operating 
subsidy that a community center could require. Participants agreed the concept warrants additional 
community input and future discussion, but no location or funding plan was selected. 
No decisions were made. 
4. Other/Additional Subject Matters from Commissioners - ORS 192.640(1) 
The City and County discussed additional collaboration on housing and development. Topics included 
bringing developers, builders and realtors together with City and County planning/building staff; the City’s 
development task force; workforce housing; Middle-Income Revolving Loan (MIRL/MERL) funding 
described by the City; and comparing permit-processing practices and timelines. The parties agreed to 
encourage coordination between their planning/building departments. Commissioner Richardson also 
expressed concern about limited City engagement on prior long-range planning for North Valley sewer and 
water extensions; City representatives agreed to work on improving communication. The group agreed to 
revisit public lands, car camping and the youth community center at the next monthly meeting, along with 
an update on planning/building collaboration. No formal decisions were made. 
The meeting adjourned at 12:07 p.m.
