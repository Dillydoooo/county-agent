Executive Summary 
This document outlines organizational improvements across staffing, clinical programs, 
equipment modernization, and community lntegratlon, demonstrating a high-performing 
EMS system aligned with national standards and regional needs. 
Staffing and Workforce Development 
AMR maintains 42 full-time and 45 part-time personnel including RNs, paramedics, and 
BMTs. A 24-hour supervisor model Is ln place with a structured training pipeline for future 
leaders. A Field Training Officer (FTO) program ensures clinical consistency. Medical 
oversight is provided by Physician Supervisor Adam Wurstle. Recruitment and workforce 
development are supported through student programs and paramedic scholarships. 
Operational Capacity 
The system operates seven staffed ambulances at peak capacity with four reserve units and 
one four-wheel-drive unit for wildland response. Specialty resources include a Mass 
Casualty Incident trailer and a Rapid Emergency Medical (REM) UTV and trailer for off-road 
response. 
Clinical Excellence Programs 
AMR maintains CAAS accredltatlcn, the gold standard in ambulance services. Clinical 
programs include Critical Care Transport (CCT) teams staffed by RNs and specialty 
paramedics, Stroke coordination with Asante, STEMI response programs including regional 
equipment support, and sepsis protocol integration. Participation In the CARES registry 
demonstrates cardiac arrest survival rates 40% above the national average. 
Technology and Innovation 
Electronic Patient Care Reporting ( ePCR) is Integrated with Josephine County 911 CAD 
systems. Advanced cardiac monitoring (LP-iS), medication Infusion pumps, and video- 
assisted airway systems support Improved patient outcomes and clinical accuracy.

regional trauma advisory boards, and multiple local committees, The organization

outreach includes high school CERT support and college clinical programs.
