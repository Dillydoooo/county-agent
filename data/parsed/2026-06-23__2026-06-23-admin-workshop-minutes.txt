2. Department Business 
a. Sheriff 
1. Adult Jail and Juvenile Detention Levy 
Dave Daniel, Sheriff, and Scott Hyde, Community Corrections appeared before the Board to discuss renewal of the 
existing Adult Jail and Juvenile Detention Levy. Sheriff Daniel explained that the current levy rate of $0.93 per 
$1,000 of assessed value has remained unchanged for ten years. While the levy has historically funded both jail 
and juvenile detention operations, increasing operational costs have created a structural funding deficit that can 
no longer be sustained solely through levy revenues.  
Sheriff Daniel and Director Hyde proposed renewing the levy at its current rate while utilizing temporary 
General Fund support for juvenile detention operations. Director Hyde explained that approximately $0.89 of 
the levy would be required to maintain current jail operations, leaving approximately $0.04 available for 
juvenile detention. Additional funding would be needed to sustain juvenile services until a long-term funding 
solution could be developed. Discussion included the possibility of establishing a future juvenile services 
district, timelines required to create and fund a district, voter sentiment regarding taxation, and the importance 
of maintaining public safety services throughout the County.  
Commissioners discussed the financial realities facing the County, the impact of inflation on operating 
expenses, staffing stability, and the challenges associated with sustaining essential public safety services. 
Sheriff Daniel emphasized that the proposal would not increase taxes but would instead renew the existing levy 
rate currently paid by taxpayers. Director Hyde noted that closure of juvenile detention services could result in 
significant operational and staffing challenges that would take considerable time and resources to rebuild. The 
Board agreed to continue discussions with legal counsel and staff prior to the July deadline for ballot measures.  
b. Recreation 
1. Grant Agreement, HB5204 Horse Racing $1,000.000.00 
Tamra Martin, Recreation Director, presented a grant agreement associated with House Bill 5204, which appropriated 
$1,000,000 to counties that host horse racing activities. Director Martin explained that Josephine County's circumstances 
differ from other counties because horse racing operations are conducted through the Southern Oregon Horse Racing 
Association rather than directly by the County. As a result, the County will largely function as a pass-through entity for 
grant administration, while the Oregon Racing Commission retains oversight authority for project approvals and 
expenditures.  
 
Director Martin stated that approved projects include improvements to facilities utilized primarily for horse racing activities, 
including restroom upgrades beneath the grandstands and drainage improvements associated with horse racing operations. 
She explained that continued horse racing activities benefit the Fairgrounds through lease revenue, increased public 
attendance, and additional exposure to Fairgrounds events and facilities.  Commissioner Martin moved to approve Grant 
Agreement, HB5204 Horse Racing $1,000.000.00. Commissioner Richardson seconded the motion. Upon roll call; 
Commissioner Richardson – yes, Commissioner Martin – yes, Commissioner Smith – yes. Motion carried 3-0. 
 
3. BOARD BUSINESS 
a. Union Negotiations Authorization and Criteria

Michelle Shaw, Human Resources, Natalie Eckhardt-Doupe, Human Resources Analyst and Rob Brandes, Public 
Works met with the Board regarding upcoming labor negotiations and expiration of the current collective 
bargaining agreement on June 30, 2026. Director Shaw explained that a bargaining unit representative had 
requested a one-year contract extension and that negotiations must begin immediately to meet upcoming 
deadlines. 
 
Staff discussed historical negotiation practices, anticipated bargaining topics, including cost-of-living adjustments 
and healthcare contributions, and the need to establish negotiating authority and parameters. Commissioners 
reviewed potential negotiation objectives, discussed the importance of maintaining good-faith bargaining 
practices, and considered the benefits of utilizing internal staff resources rather than outside legal counsel. Staff 
reported they would continue gathering historical bargaining information and return with additional 
recommendations as negotiations progress. The Board expressed support for staff continuing negotiations and 
gathering additional information before final terms are presented for Board consideration. 
 
b. Silverbrook Phase 2 Plat Map Subdivision 
The Board reviewed the Silverbrook Phase 2 Plat Map Subdivision. Discussion noted that the subdivision had already 
received City approval and would add additional residential housing units within the Fruitdale area. Commissioners 
acknowledged the importance of increasing housing opportunities and reviewed the plat documentation provided for 
approval. 
Commissioner Martin moved to approve Silverbrook Phase 2Plat Map Subdivision. Commissioner Richardson seconded 
the motion. Upon roll call; Commissioner Richardson – yes, Commissioner Martin – yes, Commissioner Smith – yes. Motion 
carried 3-0. 
c. Proposed Removal of Murphy Dam on the Applegate River 
Chair Smith discussed the proposed removal of Murphy Dam on the Applegate River and recent information 
received regarding permits obtained through the County Planning Department. Commissioners reviewed 
concerns regarding the potential loss of irrigation infrastructure, groundwater recharge benefits, agricultural 
impacts, firefighting water availability, and increased operational costs for local landowners. Discussion also 
included Water Watch of Oregon's involvement, the regulatory review process, and opportunities for public 
comment before the Department of Environmental Quality. 
Commissioner Martin expressed support for a draft of opposition letter prepared for distribution to regulatory 
agencies and interested parties. Commissioner Richardson indicated he would like additional time to review the 
matter but supported continuing discussion. Commissioners agreed that County concerns should be formally 
communicated and that the letter should be distributed to all relevant agencies and stakeholders. The Board 
approved moving forward with distribution of the letter without objection 
d. Anne G. Basker Auditorium Rental Application – Josephine County Court- Staff 7/14/26 
The Board reviewed an auditorium rental application submitted on behalf of the Josephine County Circuit Court 
for use of the Anne G. Basker Auditorium. The application was approved without objection.
