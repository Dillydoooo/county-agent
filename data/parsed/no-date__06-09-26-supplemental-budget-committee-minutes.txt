1) Open Meeting – Gary Richardson, Budget Committee Chair 
Commissioner Richardson opened the meeting at 9:00 a.m.  
2) Declaration of Potential Conflicts of Interest 
Budget Committee members reviewed the agenda and declared no potential conflicts of interest related to 
matters on the agenda. 
3) Budget Officer presents the Fiscal Year 2025-26 Supplemental Budget 
Arthur O’Hare, Budget Officer, presented the proposed Fiscal Year 2025-2026 Supplemental Budget. 
O'Hare explained that the supplemental budget was primarily a year-end housekeeping measure intended to 
adjust appropriations, recognize grant revenues, and transfer funds between budget categories as needed to 
remain in compliance with Oregon Budget Law. O'Hare noted that Josephine County's charter requires 
Budget Committee review of supplemental budgets prior to consideration by the Board of County 
Commissioners at a public hearing. The supplemental budget would subsequently be published and 
presented to the Board for consideration on June 24, 2026.  Mr. O'Hare reviewed the proposed adjustments 
and explained that the supplemental budget increased the Fiscal Year 2025-2026 budget by $1,137,000 with 
no change to countywide full-time equivalent positions. The largest adjustment consisted of $1,062,000 in 
grant funding for Community Corrections programs, including Family Treatment Court and Behavioral 
Health Deflection programs. Additional changes included a $20,000 transfer from Animal Shelter revenues 
to the Animal Shelter Building Reserve Fund and a $55,000 increase associated with sewer service costs at 
the North Valley Industrial Park.  Discussion occurred regarding the North Valley Industrial Park sewer 
subsidy, replacement and outfitting of Sheriff's Office patrol vehicles, Community Corrections grant 
funding, court security overtime expenditures, Animal Shelter reserve funding, and Sheriff forfeiture fund 
appropriations. O'Hare explained that several adjustments involved transfers between budget categories 
rather than increases in overall expenditures and reviewed requirements associated with supplemental budget 
procedures under Oregon Budget Law. Community Justice representatives provided additional information 
regarding state grant funding and explained that funding levels for several programs were not known when 
the original budget was prepared.  Committee members discussed contingency usage, grant funding 
assumptions, reserve balances, and statutory requirements governing supplemental budgets.  
4) Public Comment (limit of three minutes each) 
Stephen Fitch, Josephine County, spoke regarding government accountability, citizen participation in 
government, and concerns related to public officials and public trust. 
5) Deliberate on Supplemental Budget 
The budget committee deliberated on the supplemental budget, and asked Mr. O’Hare clarifying questions.  
6) Motion to Approve Supplemental Budget 
Mr. Smith moved to approve the Supplemental Budget for Josephine County to increase the original 
1137000. The motion was seconded by Ms. Yetter. Upon Roll Call- Ms. Yetter – Yes, Mr. Collins – Yes, Ms. 
Martin – Yes, Mr. Smith – Yes, Mr. Kirby – Yes, Mr. Richardson – Yes. Motion carried 6-0. 
Mr. Smith moved to delegate approval of the Budget Committee minutes to the Board of County 
Commissioners. The motion was seconded by Ms. Yetter. Upon Roll Call- Ms. Yetter – Yes, Mr. Collins – 
Yes, Ms. Martin – Yes, Mr. Smith – Yes, Mr. Kirby – Yes, Mr. Richardson – Yes. Motion carried 6-0. 
7) Adjourn 
The meeting adjourned at 9:29 a.m.
