Legal Counsel Update: September 24, 2026 
10:00 a.m. – Board of Commissioners Conference Room 
Attending: Commissioners Ron A. Smith and Gary L. Richardson; Wally Hicks, Legal Counsel; 
Leah Harper, Assistant Legal Counsel; and Nikole Guzman, Recorder. Commissioner Colene M. 
Martin was absent. 
Legal Counsel Update 
1. Declaration of Potential Conflicts of Interest 
Commissioners Richardson and Smith each declared no potential conflicts of interest. 
Commissioner Martin was absent and did not participate in the meeting. 
2. Approval to increase spending limit for Snell Wilmer (surplus proceeds litigation) 
Wally Hicks, Legal Counsel, reported that the surplus-proceeds litigation being defended by 
Snell Wilmer had settled the preceding day. To conclude outstanding legal billing, he 
recommended that the Board authorize a spending limit of $40,000 for legal services during 
July through September 2026. Counsel explained that invoices were still being compiled and 
that this amount was an authorized ceiling rather than an accounting of expenses already 
incurred. He advised that the limit was needed for Finance to process the remaining 
invoices. 
With the two Commissioners present and without objection, the Board approved the $40,000 
spending limit for Snell Wilmer and directed that the authorization be communicated to 
Finance. 
3. RFP for contracted attorney services 
Wally Hicks presented a proposed Request for Proposals (RFP) for contracted attorney 
services in anticipation of Assistant Legal Counsel Leah Harper’s upcoming retirement. He 
proposed sending an inquiry statewide to selected law firms and other legal-service 
providers to evaluate options for obtaining general county counsel support and specialized 
expertise on an as-needed or retainer basis. Hicks stated that a contracted approach could 
provide cost savings and allow the County to draw upon different legal specialties. 
Commissioner Richardson discussed his experience with a similar retainer arrangement at 
the school district and supported exploring multiple specialized providers as appropriate. 
Counsel indicated that responses would be brought back to the Board for review before any 
firm or contract was selected. The Board also thanked Harper for her service and briefly 
discussed whether she might be available for limited contract assistance on unfinished 
matters after her retirement and a vacation; no such arrangement was approved. 
The Commissioners present expressed support for proceeding with the RFP process. No 
formal motion was made and no contract or provider was approved at this meeting.
